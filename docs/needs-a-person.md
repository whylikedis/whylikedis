# Needs a person

A card lands here after its rounds of writing and review end without reaching `verified`. Agents
stop at that point: going round again invites cutting an awkward fact or padding with a weak
source. Each row says exactly what is missing and where it might be found.

A person settles a row by reading the source and giving the page and the words, or by deciding
the card should say less.
